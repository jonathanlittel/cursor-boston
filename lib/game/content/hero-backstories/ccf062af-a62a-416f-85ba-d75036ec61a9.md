## Chapter 1 — The Chalk Guard and the Cold Ground

They gave Pellumen twenty-five tiles and called it a kingdom.

He spent the first forty turns on food. Not grain — potatoes. Ugly, reliable, hard to burn. The veterans laughed. Potatoes were a peasant hedge, not a war chest. Pellumen wrote down their names.

The next sixty turns went to barracks, a frontier push north, and a river tile that a silent neighbor had been eyeing. He fortified it on a Tuesday and never heard from them again.

By turn one hundred he had a border, a harvest, and a file of infantry he called the Chalk Guard. They sharpened their blades each morning on the ridge. They ate well.

He enlisted with the Blue because the math was simple: seven Seals, one lottery, a world that would eventually end. He intended to be fed and standing when it did.

The fog waited. So did the potatoes.
